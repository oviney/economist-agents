Editorial Review Rubric

Applied to every post the pipeline generates, before publication. Five blocking gates, six weighted dimensions, one reproducible verdict. The audience checks numbers, so the review checks them first.

Blocking gates

G1
Source resolvability
Every figure resolves to a real locatable document. Unreachable equals unverified equals blocked.
G2
Citation fidelity
The source supports that exact claim, without inflating its sample, scope, or confidence.
G3
Arithmetic integrity
Every calculation reproduces. Units consistent, denominators stated, rounding not thesis-biased.
G4
No fabrication
No invented quotes, people, companies, metrics, or studies. Indistinguishable counts as invented.
G5
Evidence currency
Per claim, not per reference list: has the same publisher since revised, reversed, or narrowed the finding the argument rests on?

One gate failure blocks publication regardless of weighted score

Weighted dimensions

25PCT
Evidence density and quality

A 5: claims rest on primary sources. Sample sizes and study limits appear in the text. The post knows which direction its own evidence is wrong.

20PCT
Falsifiability and honesty

A 5: names the conditions under which the thesis fails, presents the strongest opposing case in its own best form, attaches explicit confidence to contested claims.

15PCT
Thesis originality and structure

A 5: a claim a well-read practitioner has not encountered, argued in a line rather than a list. Delete any section and the argument should visibly weaken.

15PCT
Practitioner actionability

A 5: a named action, a named owner role, and a named measurement that would show in six weeks whether it worked.

15PCT
Voice fidelity

A 5: quantitative by default, vendor-neutral, skeptical of consensus including its own priors. Contrarianism is not skepticism and caps this dimension at 2.

10PCT
Craft and packaging

A 5: clean prose, no LLM tells, and a title that claims exactly what the body delivers. Over-promise spends the trust the brand runs on.

Calibration discipline

A competent post that breaks no new ground lands near 70. Reserve 90 and above for a post someone else would cite. If most posts score above 85, the rubric is broken, not the pipeline.

Overclaiming and hedging-until-unfalsifiable score equally low on dimension two. They are the same failure of nerve.

Every finding carries a confidence percentage. A claim flagged at 60 percent and one flagged at 95 percent must not read the same way to the author.

LLM tells, flagged under craft

  • Scene-setting abstraction as the opening move
  • Negation-elevation: not just a testing problem, a trust problem
  • Rule-of-three padding where two or four would be honest
  • Empty register words: leverage, delve, landscape, robust, seamless, crucial
  • Symmetrical section lengths, meaning the outline drove the content
  • A conclusion that restates the introduction without moving
  • Stacked hedging: may potentially contribute to
  • Fake precision, an estimate carried to two decimals
